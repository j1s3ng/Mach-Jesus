# Apex and gap handling — local review

**Status: `READY_FOR_LOCAL_REVIEW`.** The main `chase-004` completed a full reference circuit and closed in sustained P1 at **235.1 s**, with all 20 cars active and zero overlap samples, track-limit violations or resets. The selected clear-track calibration and final-source controlled middle pass are also closed. Nine runtime overlays are frozen from `chase-004`. All four review videos passed full decoding and visual review of sampled encoded frames.

The aim is to use the full generated road for outside-entry, inside-apex, outside-exit cornering, while permitting safe passes on either side and through a reachable gap between two cars. Evidence here separates clear-track corner execution, a controlled middle pass, and traffic passing. Both-side capability does not imply that both sides were exercised in the 20-car chase.

The selected clear run, `wide8-calibrated-001`, completed one standing lap in **189.908 s**, compared with the earlier narrow-line clear lap's **196.841 s**. The new run had zero two-wheel-center violations in 9,497 supervisor checks at 50 Hz, a minimum of two centers on track, zero collision samples and no pose resets. It used exactly two contacts for 18.76 s and captured the mapped nominal path for 99.315% of measured time.

The declared custom rule counts at least two of the four wheel contact centers inside the actual generated tarmac and white-line triangle strip. Kerbs do not count. Centers are computed from the rear-axle pose with a 3.2 m wheelbase and ±0.85 m lateral placement. Diagonal-only two-center cases are legal under the raw threshold and reported separately. This is a simulator approximation, not a full tire contact-patch model or FIA certification. The final geometry repair uses a 0.6 m same-side-pair planning reserve; a separate 0.18 m inward-center check is an additional audit metric. Full-car clearance still governs traffic collision checks.

The nominal path follows actual mapped XY and its tangent. Constant-heading passing candidates use their own conservative forward bounds; clamping the mapped route to a different tangent's passing interval would change the route incorrectly. Gap ranking can use acceleration-reachable pace over a 2.5 s horizon, preventing a distant peak-speed advantage from dominating an immediately usable pass. Perception corridors were widened to 14 m from earlier 7/6 m settings. Original progress, finish line and ranking geometry remain authoritative.

The unchanged factory vehicle is 880 kg, 400 kW, with friction coefficient 1.5. The selected planner uses a finite 100 m/s ceiling and software lateral/braking/acceleration budgets of 8/8/5 m/s². These are software planning settings, not measured hard limits. The speed-dependent command-response calibration uses `K = 0.002 s²/m`; the preceding observational fit estimated 0.00213 s²/m. Steering commands were observed alongside motion, without measured road-wheel angle or tire forces. This analysis identifies neither a causal braking correction nor real F1 equivalence. See [the steering summary](evidence/steering_response_summary.json).

The geometry seed reached its finite evaluation limit without convergence. Direct lap-time refinement also exhausted its budget and was rejected because it worsened the input estimate. The chosen route uses explicit apex-flow geometry, physical-arc-length XY smoothing and local tire-pair repair. No converged solver result or global optimum is claimed. Dense planned-pose checks are reported separately from the actual clear-lap audit in [the geometry summary](evidence/geometry_checks.json).
