# Trajectory planning and trail braking — local review

**Status: `READY_FOR_LOCAL_REVIEW`.** Zero soft-risk cost is selected for local review after the declared comparison and three fresh physical fixtures. It was faster and had lower steering-command variation than risk weight 0.25. The older feedforward baseline remains faster at the first circuit. This is a bounded engineering choice, not a universal optimum.

The controller combines conditional brake release with a filtered longitudinal-demand proxy for steering allowance. Three-second forecasts and hard credibility gates remain enabled, while the soft forecast penalty is **off** after the negative cost ablation. No new hard-credible track, veto or trajectory-emergency intervention occurred in the selected run, so no forecast-based control benefit is claimed.

Zero cost met the predeclared preference rule: its first circuit was 5.160 s earlier and command variation per second 12.12% lower than risk 0.25, with qualified safety. It still crossed 4.533 s later than the older baseline. Its lead continued from 96.2 s through the stop; the later minimum-progress lead-hold gate is not the first-lead time. Stop times are not one-lap times, and no 20-lap victory is claimed.

Against the older baseline, selected-run command variation fell 2.83%, rate RMS 3.44%, acceleration RMS 5.94%, and command RMS above 1 Hz 14.17%. There were no intervals exceeding the configured slew setting. Method switches fell from 200 to 136, and nominal capture rose from 9.69% to 12.07%. Body-yaw acceleration RMS nevertheless increased from 0.88569 to 1.01854 rad/s². These recorded commands and simulator-motion derivatives do not establish that every motion is smoother. [The comparison figure](figures/traffic_ablation.png) ([PDF](figures/traffic_ablation.pdf)) and [full results](evidence/results.json) retain the tradeoffs.

All 20 cars stayed active with zero native overlaps, track or inward-margin violations, and resets. The hero retained at least two wheel centers; rivals retained four. The custom rule counts wheel centers on generated tarmac or white lines; kerbs are excluded. Distinct-rival crossings counted 16 left and three right passes. Observed 5 Hz body-envelope minima were **0.550 m** for the whole field and **1.281 m** for the hero, versus baseline's 0.927 and 1.907 m. These sampled distances show no clearance improvement, and an already zero-overlap baseline leaves no demonstrated contact-count reduction.

Only eight net brake-release ticks, totaling **0.16 s**, occurred in traffic; that tiny activation cannot explain the whole-race outcome. Earlier clear release trials were faster but had worse pedal and steering variation. The shared-source stability clear test completed in 188.787226 s with mixed command, pedal and body-motion results. Detailed [clear tradeoffs](evidence/clear_tradeoffs.json), [stability checks](evidence/stability_candidate.json) and [the failed combined case](evidence/combined_failed_case.json) remain available. That failure had zero release changes; its chronology suggests a steering-budget contributor, not a proved single cause.

Brake release acts only on an approved captured route. Its checked profile-floor invariant applies to **shaped/released samples**, not every baseline tick. The steering estimator uses a five-sample median and 0.10 s exponential estimate, retaining immediate positive-acceleration, mandatory-deceleration and actual-final-brake demand floors. Final-command reconstruction verified 143 nonzero mandatory-brake samples and 470 samples at brake command 0.88 without a delayed floor or zero steering cap. Friction, profiles, weights and existing guards remain unchanged; these are command-model checks, not measured tire forces.

**Known forecast defect:** at recorded world time 37.2 s, one coasted, noncredible track has one 2.8 s forecast center **1.777 m below its lower progress bound**. Exact frozen-source replay reproduces the outside-road metric/endpoint-stepping issue. The raw snapshot is retained without clamping or deletion. All soft costs, hard vetoes and trajectory-emergency requests were zero, so this forecast imposed no new control restriction. [The forecast audit and exception](evidence/predictor_audits.json) explicitly reject an all-bounds-enclose-their-centers claim. At three seconds, matched-origin median center error was 8.805 m and center containment 77.92%. This is not body containment or calibrated probability; observed tracks are not omniscient knowledge of unseen cars.
