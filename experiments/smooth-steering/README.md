# Steering smoothing — local review

Historical media links below resolve to the [local archive catalog](../../docs/archive.md). Source/evidence receipts retain their original artifact identities.

**Status: `READY_FOR_LOCAL_REVIEW`.** The feedforward-only **1 m setting is selected** after closed clear laps, a full-circuit traffic chase and a controlled middle pass. Its clear lap was **189.036099 s**, 0.871608 s faster than baseline, with lower command variation, rate RMS and acceleration RMS. Traffic gains were modest and mixed. Both new review videos passed full decoding and encoded-frame visual QA.

Apply this two-file overlay **after** the [apex-and-gap package](../apex-and-gap/README.md), whose manifest SHA256 is `01b53aa69f489c71ee2fdccad5053e76e20d2552d5e0186bc3e5384279ab1ef1`. A compatible base simulator checkout and the unchanged external reference, map, line plan and model artifacts are still required. No NPZ archives, weights or raw traces are distributed here.

The change averages only direct-branch map-curvature feedforward over a symmetric Gaussian window in physical route distance. The selected `path_tracking.feedforward_curvature_smoothing_sigma_m` is `1.0`; the finite comparison also tested `2.0`. Its unit is metres of Gaussian standard deviation, with support extending to three standard deviations on either side. The default `0.0` reproduces legacy commands and metadata on 608 saved clear/traffic inputs, including 15 guarded inputs. This is a spatial map calculation with no temporal filter state.

Current-pose heading and lateral feedback, pursuit steering, the approved passing lane, traffic guards, steering-rate bounds and final lateral/stability clamps remain active. The route XY, heading, speed profiles, vehicle physics, learned weights and custom track rule are unchanged. A smoothing window touching a parallel-curve cusp is bypassed; the cache holds one current route/lane table. Twenty-one targeted tests passed. These tests and saved-input replay are separate from physical qualification; see [the test receipt](evidence/test_receipt.json).

The second overlay changes telemetry only. It allows per-entry `telemetry_period_ms`; the hero records each 20 ms control step while entries without that setting retain the global 100 ms logging default. It does not change control timing or vehicle behavior.

The closed `baseline-clear-001` reproduces the earlier clear lap exactly: **189.907707 s**, zero actual two-wheel-center violations, no overlaps or resets, and a minimum of two wheel centers on track. At all 1,900 common saved timestamps, steering command, speed, progress and lane values match the earlier recording exactly. The extra variation observed at 50 Hz comes from the recording cadence.

Against that matched baseline, the selected 1 m clear lap reduced command variation/time by **17.00%**, command-rate RMS by **25.79%**, command-acceleration RMS by **33.18%**, and command RMS above 1 Hz by **24.43%**. Confirmed 0.005 rad excursion reversals fell from 282 to 232, and post-clamp intervals exceeding the configured slew setting fell from 21 to six. Nominal-path capture was 99.312%. Seventeen of 18 fixed original-map corner anchors were faster, with a mean gain of 0.644 km/h; this is not an all-corner improvement claim.

A separate diagnostic from actual simulator rear-axle heading also found lower body-yaw acceleration RMS, 1.3671 to 1.1827 rad/s², and lower yaw-rate RMS above 1 Hz, 0.029545 to 0.025064 rad/s. These use body-heading finite differences and an offline measurement filter, not measured road-wheel angles or tire forces. The source hash and all three arms are retained in the clear-selection evidence.

All three clear laps had a minimum of two contact centers and zero 50 Hz track-limit violations, overlaps or resets, with strict source pins verified. The 2 m setting was rejected: its 190.202650 s lap was slower than baseline, command-acceleration RMS rose 14.34%, reversals rose to 292, and slew-exception intervals rose to 25, despite a 42.55% reduction in command RMS above 1 Hz. The selection uses one observed lap per setting, not multiple seeds or traffic performance. See [the clear-selection audit](evidence/clear_selection.json).

The diagnosis identifies three sources of command ripple. Map-curvature feedforward varies rapidly even while the clear nominal path is captured. A final speed/friction proxy clamp can also shrink abruptly after the slew limiter: the 50 Hz baseline contains 21 intervals above the configured 1 rad/s slew setting, with a maximum command rate of 3.305 rad/s. Traffic adds changes of route and control method, plus larger feedback corrections. The present candidate tests only the feedforward source; the clamp and transition effects remain part of the measured result. See [the sanitized diagnosis](evidence/diagnosis.json).

The separate 50 Hz traffic baseline closed at 238.22 s with all 20 cars clean. It has a documented **GUI-layout-only source-pin exception**: Webots changed three encoded values on the `.wbproj` `perspectives` line. The original launcher source-verification error remains preserved. The independent adjudication verified the other 63 of 64 frozen source/asset pins and all 20 actors' runtime imports, model/detector identity, rules and safety. It is qualified with that exact exception; it is not labeled a strict all-source-pin pass. See [the sanitized adjudication](evidence/baseline_traffic_ui_exception.json). Future prepared snapshots exclude editor layout state before pinning.

The selected feedforward-only traffic run closed cleanly at **232.38 s**, compared with 238.22 s for baseline, and first saved P1 was 88.2 versus 99.0 s. All 20 actors remained active with zero overlap, two-center violations, resets or failures. The candidate passed all 62 frozen source/asset pins strictly; its hero had a minimum of two contacts in 11,620 supervisor checks and used exactly two for 0.74 s. Independent crossings counted 19 left and zero right passes, versus baseline's 16 left and three right. These are single matched-scenario runs with different closed-loop trajectories, not a formal 20-lap win or multi-seed result.

Whole-traffic command variation/time fell **5.37%**, rate RMS **6.42%**, acceleration RMS **1.30%**, and command RMS above 1 Hz **7.01%**. Body-yaw acceleration RMS fell about 16.89%. However, command reversals increased from 643 to 662, method switches from 152 to 200, and nominal capture fell from 11.72% to 9.69%. This supports a modest traffic change, not globally smooth traffic control. See [the final traffic audit](evidence/traffic_comparison.json) and [the paired metrics figure](figures/traffic_comparison.png) ([PDF](figures/traffic_comparison.pdf)).

All three actors retained four wheel centers on track with zero overlap, track violations or resets. This documents one actual guarded encounter, not every possible emergency-avoidance response.
