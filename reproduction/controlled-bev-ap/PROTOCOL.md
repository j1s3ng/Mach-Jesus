# Predeclared simulated DEV BEV AP protocol

Population: all 6,144 frozen known development frames, including empty frames; 8,155 labeled cars within 50 m and with >=3 measured returns. Six fixed model predictions use shared post-NMS score floor >=0.1, NMS IoU 0.2 and max 83; excluded boxes are kept as ignore regions. Predictions are globally sorted by descending score, with scene/frame/original prediction index deterministic tie-break. For each prediction greedily match the highest BEV IoU >=0.5 among unmatched eligible ground truths in its frame; a matched eligible target is one TP and cannot be matched again. Otherwise ignore a prediction if it overlaps any excluded GT at BEV IoU >=0.5; excluded GT can ignore multiple predictions and never contributes to the recall denominator. Remaining predictions are FP, including duplicates. Eligible matches take precedence over ignore regions. Include the complete eligible denominator and all retained scored predictions; interpolated AP is the mean over 101 recall levels 0, .01, ..., 1 of the maximal precision at any achieved recall >= that level (zero where no such point exists). Interpolation is global, after removing ignored predictions. BEV means oriented XY footprint, ignoring height and front/back direction. Original and adapted models share a postprocessing floor; scores below 0.1 and suppressed pre-NMS boxes are unrecoverable. An excluded GT is ignored on overlap irrespective of why it was excluded; out-of-range predictions were already absent from the cached model outputs. Label this fixed-postprocessing IoU AP, never official KITTI/nuScenes/A2RL AP. Known correlated DEV data and a single training seed; no closed-loop or fresh-generalization claim.
